Note: most functionality can be visibly interacted with (i.e. UI), this doc is just to highlight some specific less-obvious functionality.



[ Loading a Level ]
- by default, the system should load 'level0' first
- if not, the dropdown menu will allow you to load a level from /Resources/Levels/

- when clicking "load", the scene will "reload"
	- to access the now loaded level, click the small 'x' at the corner of the Load menu!


[ Editing Protected Tiles ]
- on the left, click the shield icon
- From the dropdown menu, choose the corresponding layer that you wish to view/adjust
	- "Protected" tiles will have a blue highlight around them
	- press the "up" arrow to toggle a tile's "protected" status
